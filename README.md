# suraksha-sethu
Suraksha Sethu – Your Digital Safety Bridge

Project Description

Suraksha Sethu is an AI-powered, multilingual cyber scam detection and protection platform designed to protect elderly people, rural communities, and users with limited digital literacy from online fraud.

Key Features

- AI Scam Detection: Analyzes suspicious messages and identifies common scam patterns.
- Multilingual Support: Provides simple safety guidance in Indian languages.
- Smart Safety Action Planner: Suggests practical steps based on the detected scam or fraud situation.
- User-Friendly Interface: Offers an accessible and easy-to-use experience for all age groups.
- Cybercrime Awareness: Educates users about OTP fraud, phishing, fake banking messages, and online scams.
- Emergency Guidance: Directs users to official cybercrime reporting channels when needed.

Problem Statement

Elderly and rural users are frequently targeted by cybercriminals and may struggle to distinguish genuine communications from fraudulent ones.

Our Solution

Suraksha Sethu combines AI-based scam analysis, multilingual alerts, and actionable safety recommendations to help users recognize potential cyber threats and make safer digital decisions.

Mission

To bridge the digital safety gap and make online transactions and digital communication safer for everyone.

Technology

AI/ML, modern web technologies, and multilingual processing.

Project Goal

To build a safer digital India through accessible, intelligent, and user-friendly cyber fraud awareness and protection.
