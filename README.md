# suraksha-sethu
🛡️ Suraksha Sethu — Your Digital Safety Bridge

Suraksha Sethu is an AI-powered, multilingual cyber scam detection and protection platform designed to protect elderly people, rural communities, and users with limited digital literacy from online fraud.

✨ Key Features

- 🤖 AI-powered scam message analysis and risk assessment
- 🌐 Multilingual interface supporting 22 scheduled Indian languages and English
- 📸 Screenshot-based scam detection using OCR, when configured
- 🎙️ Voice interaction and audio scam analysis, when supported
- 🚨 Emergency assistance with India's cybercrime helpline 1930
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Trusted family contact support
- 📚 Cyber safety awareness and practical prevention tips
- 🔒 Privacy-focused design and scam history management

🎯 Our Mission

To make digital banking and online communication safer, simpler, and more accessible for every Indian.

🛠️ Technology

React, TypeScript, AI/ML integrations, and modern responsive web technologies.

Note: AI, authentication, OCR, and speech features depend on the availability and configuration of their respective services.
